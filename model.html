NX-1 Highlights.

Young. Urban. Electric.

With its progressive interpretation of classic design, the all-electric NX-1 shows at first glance exactly what it's made of.

Driver Experience.

The highly digital cockpit with an individually configurable curved display offers operating comfort and individual control.

Aerodynamic Efficiency.

Carbon fiber elements and active aerodynamics ensure maximum downforce and minimal drag.

Minimalist Form.

A striking silhouette paired with sharp, purposeful lines creating an unmistakable presence anywhere.

Active Air Intakes.

Electronically controlled flaps open only when cooling is required, significantly reducing drag during high-speed cruising.

Carbon Fiber Diffuser.

The aggressive rear diffuser accelerates underbody airflow, creating a low-pressure zone that sucks the car to the tarmac.

Adaptive Rear Wing.

Deploying automatically at 120 km/h, the rear wing acts as an airbrake under heavy deceleration while optimizing downforce through corners.

NX High-Voltage Battery Guarantee.

Every all-electric NX-1 is delivered with a worldwide guarantee against material and manufacturing defects on the high-voltage battery, which is valid for eight years or up to a mileage of 160,000 km.1

We also guarantee the residual capacity of the high-voltage battery: 80% of the original net battery capacity is guaranteed for up to three years or 60,000 km1, after which we guarantee at least 70% residual capacity until the end of the HV battery guarantee.

1Whichever comes first.
